# Release Notes -- v0.2.0

> Released: 2026-10-12

Two of the nine Spirits have new figures. Computation is now the Engine,
Babbage's engine worked by hand, and Knowledge is now the Owl-bearer. The
design record in `docs/` is rewritten as one complete document.

## What changed

**Computation and Knowledge.** The Engine replaces the Architect as the
default Spirit of Computation; the Architect remains as a variant. The
Owl-bearer replaces the robed figure for Knowledge, which read as priestly;
the robed figure is kept in `archive/knowledge-priestess/`. Both new models
ship at full detail on desktop and simplified to half on phones.

**Variants.** A Spirit's variant now carries its own render and card text, and
the plaque card follows whichever variant is on show.

**The design record.** `docs/README.md` now covers where everything lives, The
Gilded Circuit's layout, city, sky, light, craft and scale, all nine Spirits
with their allegories, the Core, the motifs, the posters and the first
studies. Several plaque cards were corrected to match the current figures and
plinths.

**Citation.** The README carries the Zenodo DOI badge and `CITATION.cff`
carries the concept DOI, `10.5281/zenodo.23307133`, which always resolves to
the newest archived release.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. The plaza on GitHub Pages updates on push to `main`.
