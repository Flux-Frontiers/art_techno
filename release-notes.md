# Release Notes -- v0.3.0

> Released: 2026-10-13

The city around the plaza gains landmarks and the sky becomes real. The Sun
Towers frame the Core, a pole tower points at Polaris, and the planets shine at
their true places. Six of the Spirits were redesigned or re-sculpted to move
them away from the classical statues they echoed.

## What changed

**The city.** A matched pair of Meshy towers, the Sun Towers, stands either
side of the Core, each crowned with the boot sun. Every other tower now wears a
boot-sun crown drawn in code, the skyline is thinned to stand the landmarks
clear, and the city dims after dark so the sky and the floodlit statues carry
the night. Due north of the floor marker, the pole tower carries a Meshy finial
whose tip stops a degree under Polaris; its height follows the sky's latitude.
Its mirror to the south is silver, so the two can be told apart.

**The sky.** The sky can follow your location, rounded and kept only in your
browser. Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn appear at their real
positions and brightness. Twilight now hides the faint stars first, so dusk and
dawn look like dusk and dawn. About's Tonight's sky reports the moon, the
active meteor showers, the planets up tonight, and where to find Polaris.

**The plaza floor.** A gold floor marker in front of the processor shows the
one spot where both alignments hold: Ad Astra's star on the Core's winged chip
looking west, and the pole tower under Polaris looking north. The artist's
signature is inlaid in the paving.

**The Spirits.** Liberty, Power, Communication and Knowledge were redesigned
away from the Statue of Liberty, Rockefeller Center's Atlas, the Greek goddess
and the 1940s working woman. Life's helix now rises into a radiating chip.
Innovation is now Eureka, a figure springing forward with a joyful cry, with
the kneeling figure kept as a variant. Ad Astra, Industry and Life were
re-sculpted as closed, full-detail models. The Engine turns a quarter round so
its machine faces the avenue. Plaque cards now show the accepted Grok renders.

**Brand and README.** `docs/brand/` holds a hero image, a social card,
wordmark lockups, section rules and a brand board, all drawn by
`docs/brand/build.py`. The README opens with the hero and ends with a citation
block.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. The plaza on GitHub Pages updates on push to `main`. The new
models are larger, so the first visit after the release downloads more.
